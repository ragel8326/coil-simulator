Off-axis Check

Field when the probe sits a radial distance ρ off the coil axis (full elliptic-integral loop field), compared with the on-axis model over the window [0, d]. Review only — not used by the optimizer.

Hz는 축 방향 프로브가 읽는 값, Hr은 프로브가 기울어졌을 때 섞여 들어가는 옆 방향 성분. ΔHz = off-axis Hz − on-axis H; max deviation is measured from the fixed target line (h1 → h2).
